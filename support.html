Playing the game

How do I play?

Five in Row (Gomoku) is played on a 15 × 15 board. Players take turns placing one stone at a time. The first to line up five or more stones in an unbroken row – horizontally, vertically or diagonally – wins. If the board fills up and nobody has five in a row, the game is a draw.

You play the cyan stones, your opponent plays pink.

How do I place a stone?

Touch the board and drag to aim – a crosshair shows exactly where your stone will land. Lift your finger to place it. Changed your mind? Slide your finger off the board before lifting and nothing is placed.

What are Classic, Blitz and Chaos?
  • Classic – pure Gomoku on a 15 × 15 board. No clock, no tricks.
  • Blitz – 10 seconds per move. The clock starts after the first stone is placed; if your time runs out, you lose. Undo and hints are not available in Blitz.
  • Chaos – random blocked cells reshape the board every game. Nobody can place a stone on a blocked cell, so plan your lines around them.

Choose the mode on the New Game screen after tapping Play.

Which opponents can I play against?
  • Easy – relaxed games that forgive your mistakes.
  • Medium – solid defence that spots most of your threats.
  • Hard – thinks many moves ahead. Bring your best game.
  • 2 Players – pass the device and play a friend face to face.

Under First Move you decide who starts: you, your opponent or random.

What do the tactical alerts mean?

During a game, call-outs highlight important patterns on the board:

  • Open three – three in a row with both ends open. Left alone, it can grow into an open four.
  • Four and open four – one move away from five. An open four has both ends free and can't be stopped.
  • Double three and four + three – two threats made with a single stone; the opponent can only block one.
  • Block the four and Danger – warnings that your opponent is threatening to win.

Call-outs ending in "!!" mark shapes that win by force unless they are answered correctly.

Prefer to read the board yourself? Turn them off in Settings → Tactical Alerts.

How do undo and hints work?
  • Against the AI you get 3 undos and 3 hints per game. With Premium they are unlimited.
  • In 2-player games undo is unlimited and hints are not available.
  • Blitz games have no undo or hints.

Note: the Purist achievement requires beating the Hard AI without using undo or hints.

How do XP, ranks and achievements work?

Every finished game against the AI earns XP. Wins are worth the most and grow with the difficulty, with bonuses for Blitz and Chaos, quick finishes and win streaks. Draws and losses still earn a little.

As you level up you climb the ranks: Novice, Apprentice, Tactician, Strategist, Expert, Master, Grandmaster and Legend. There are 9 achievements to unlock. Open Profile from the main menu to see your level, win rate, best streak, record against each AI level and achievements.

2-player games are just for fun and don't affect XP or statistics.

Free games & Premium

How many games can I play for free?

You can start up to 10 games per day for free. A game counts when it starts – including Play Again and 2-player games. The counter resets at midnight, your device's local time. The dots under the main menu show how many free games are left today.

What does Premium include?
  • Unlimited games every day – no daily limit.
  • Unlimited undos and hints in every game against the AI.

Premium is an optional auto-renewing monthly subscription. The price is shown in the app in your local currency before you confirm the purchase, and payment is charged to your Apple Account.

How do I subscribe or restore my purchase?

Tap Premium on the main menu (or Settings → Premium), then Subscribe. If you already subscribed – for example on another device or after reinstalling the app – tap Restore Purchases on the same screen. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone or iPad open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, or open the App Store, tap your account picture and choose Subscriptions.

To avoid being charged for the next period, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Premium stays active until the end of the period you've already paid for.

Can I get a refund?

All payments are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple too. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The Premium screen says "Store unavailable"

The app couldn't reach the App Store. Check your internet connection, make sure you're signed in to the App Store and that in-app purchases are allowed in Settings → Screen Time, then try again.

Data & troubleshooting

Where is my progress saved? Will it move to a new device?

Your level, XP, achievements, statistics and settings are stored only on your device – there is no account and nothing is uploaded. Progress is not synced between devices, but it is included in your device backups (iCloud or computer), so restoring a backup brings it back.

Deleting the app erases your progress. A Premium subscription is never lost: use Restore Purchases after reinstalling.

I can't hear any sound

Check that Settings → Sound Effects is on in the app. Five in Row respects the Ring/Silent switch, so sounds are muted while your device is in Silent mode. It also plays alongside your music instead of stopping it.

I don't feel any haptics

Make sure Settings → Haptics is on in the app and that System Haptics is enabled in iOS Settings → Sounds & Haptics. iPad models don't have haptic feedback.

What do I need to play?
  • An iPhone or iPad with iOS / iPadOS 17.0 or later.
  • No internet connection for playing – the game works fully offline. You only need to be online to subscribe, restore purchases or refresh your subscription status.
  • The app is available in English.
